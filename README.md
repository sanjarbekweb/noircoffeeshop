A high-end, premium single-page product site designed to showcase the intersection of modern tech-product aesthetics and traditional coffee artistry. **NOIR** is more than a landing page; it is a cinematic digital experience built with a focus on precision, motion, and minimalist sophistication.

---

## ☕ The Vision

Inspired by the "X" (formerly Twitter) official UI style and high-end fashion editorials, **NOIR** utilizes a strict monochromatic palette and sharp, geometric layouts to convey a sense of authority and luxury. The goal was to create a site that feels alive—responding to user interactions with fluid, high-performance animations while maintaining a calm, professional atmosphere.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎬 Cinematic Motion System

- **GSAP ScrollTrigger:** Every section transitions smoothly with scroll-based reveals, opacity fades, and vertical shifts.
- **Lenis Smooth Scroll:** Integrated for a decoupled, high-inertia scrolling experience that feels premium across all devices.
- **Text Reveal Engine:** Headline characters emerge word-by-word with staggered timing, creating a "cinematic intro" feel upon landing.

### 🕹️ Interactive UX

- **Magnetic Buttons:** Primary CTA buttons utilize a custom physics-based hover effect, subtly pulling toward the user's cursor.
- **Vanilla Tilt (3D Parallax):** Interactive cards in the _Services_ and _Team_ sections react to mouse movement with sophisticated 3D depth and dynamic glare filters.
- **Micro-Interactions:** Subtle border expansions, grayscale-to-color transitions, and scale-in effects on hover.

### 📐 Design System

- **Color Palette:** Pure Black (`#000`), Dark Neutrals (`#050505`), and Stark White (`#FFF`).
- **Typography:** Bold, confident sans-serif headers with wide-tracked, ultra-thin subheaders for high contrast.
- **Geometry:** Square radii (4px–6px), thin divider lines (1px), and clean grid alignment.
